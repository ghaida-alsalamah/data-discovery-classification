# Predefined native-label scoring

- DoDuo VizNet predicts one of 78 closed-set labels. Exact scoring uses the literal crosswalk in the adapter. “duration” is a broader Duration category only. “name” and “person” are not evidence of a direct personal name. “currency” does not guarantee a currency code. Unmapped predictions are misses for exact semantic scoring. The report also shows score on supported truth separately, plus unsupported truth and prediction counts. DoDuo does not provide business domain labels.
- PII Engineer outputs nine span categories. An entity type must occur in a majority of sampled non-null values to generate a column-level direct-PII candidate. Span overlap suppression and native 0.5 confidence cutoff are applied per value. Government IDs and bank accounts are scored as native coarse types. They do not directly map back to Saudi ID, Iqama, or IBAN without corroboration. No inference model receives evaluation labels.
- For fresh direct-PII metrics, the truth dictionary explicitly distinguishes direct, linked, non-personal, and uncertain. Development benchmarks use the fixed proxy of recognized direct-identifier semantic labels and personal truth. The broad personal-data metric separately evaluates all “yes” labels, including relational references. Uncertain truth is excluded from binary scoring and counted.
- OpenMetadata's standalone PII qualifier is measured with native tags and the separately applied 80% threshold. The comparison filters tags for direct types only. Its output is not a general-purpose semantic/domain classifier.
- All fingerprinted code and crosswalks are sealed before generating the fresh municipal, library, and facilities fixtures.
